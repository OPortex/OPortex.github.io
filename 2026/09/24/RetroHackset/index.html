RetroHackset

The Main Purpose of this Project was To Turn this Telephone Into a Headset to Record Guitar Riffs, And listen to Music Through it..

The Project Beginning stage was to examine the Telephone from the inside.

When we Dissamble the Telephone we see in the upper side The speaker and
on the bottom The Mic which is bellow the Hardware.

Since Now, we know Where the Speaker And MIC is Located,
we gotta cut the wires that connect them to the motherboard and Desolder it.

Stage 2 is Soldering

Now we will remove all the components except for Speaker and Mic, and Solder 2 Jumper wires to the Speaker and 2 Jumper Wires to the Mic, and Stretch it Out Through the RJ11 Gap.
It Would look like this..

Now we gotta Solder Jumper Wires to USB audio Adapter Input Metal tabs..

Before we Solder All the Jumper wires we gotta Solder Just the Audio/speaker to the USB Audio Adapter..
If we use Multi-meter we can see the Connection will be Fine, If there was no Beeps then we gotta Solder everything again.

Since in my case, It was a success. So, we gonna Solder the remaining Jumper wires to the MIC Then we move to next stage..

It would look like this..

Stage 3 Testing the Speaker & MIC

As we can see, The Computer Detect the Speaker and the Mic, here is preview of How the recording audio Sounds like (I’m Not Professional Guitarist, I used my phone to play Guitar Riffs from youtube)

Note The audio sounds good if there is distance From MIC, Since I kept The Phone too close to the MIC the Audio have some weird noise In it, But I believe Editing the Audio will remove it.. Thank you for reading
